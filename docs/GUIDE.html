GL121123 Inspector – Galaxy Tab A11 app

STUD SHOCKER MTG · drawing GL121123 rev 00 · tablet app T1.0 · complete guide from zero to live inspection
What this is: a stand-alone inspection app for the Galaxy Tab A11. It uses the tablet's own rear camera, and all measuring is done on the tablet with the same engine as the PC station. There is no laptop and no server, and after the first start it works without internet. It is an "installable web app": you install it once from Chrome, it gets an icon on the home screen and then opens like a normal app.

2. Hardware set-up (most important for accuracy)

  1. Put the LED pad on a stable table, away from windows and moving air. Switch it on at full brightness.
  2. Mount the tablet above the pad so the rear camera is over the middle of the pad, looking straight down. The tablet must be parallel to the pad (check with a spirit level app on the tablet).
  3. Height: the whole part plus about 1 cm all round must be visible. The part should fill about 60–75 % of the picture width. Closer gives better resolution. Typical height is 20–30 cm.
  4. Switch off the ceiling light directly above, or shade it. Light from above makes the part shiny and grey instead of black. A simple cardboard hood around the stand helps a lot.
  5. Clean the camera lens with a soft cloth. Dirt shows as grey blobs.
  6. Lock everything. After calibration nothing may move: not the tablet, the stand, the pad or the zoom. If anything moves, do the calibration and training again (about 10 min).
Without a backlight (part on paper, lit from above) the app still works, but edges are less sharp. Expect about 2–3× worse repeatability, especially on the diameters. The marks on your earlier test sheet also confuse the area check, so use a clean surface.

3. Put the app online once (laptop, about 10 min)

The browser allows the camera only for apps that come from a secure https:// address. So the app folder is uploaded once to a free web host. After installation the tablet keeps its own copy and does not need the host anymore. Nothing is ever sent from the tablet: pictures and results stay on the tablet.

Option A – GitHub Pages (recommended, free, permanent)

  1. Unzip GL121123_TabletApp.zip on the laptop. You get the folder GL121123_TabletApp.
  2. Go to github.com/signup and create a free account (email + password). Remember the user name, for example metaforge-qc.
  3. Top right + → New repository. Name it gl121123, choose Public and click Create repository. (Free Pages needs a public repository. The app contains no company data, only the program.)
  4. On the new page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
  5. Open the GL121123_TabletApp folder in Windows Explorer and select everything inside it with Ctrl+A: the folders docs, icons, py, runtime and the files index.html, app.js …. Drag them into the browser window. Wait until all files are listed (about 27 MB), then click Commit changes.
  6. Check that index.html is visible at the top level of the repository, not inside a sub-folder.
  7. Settings (in the repository) → Pages (left) → under "Branch" select main and /(root) → Save.
  8. Wait 1–3 minutes and refresh. The page shows "Your site is live at https://YOUR-USER.github.io/gl121123/". That is your app address. Write it down.
  9. Test it: open the address on the laptop in Chrome. The loading screen should appear and then the app, with a "NO CAMERA" or webcam picture.

Option B – Netlify Drop

  1. Open app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
  2. Drag the whole GL121123_TabletApp folder onto the page. You get an address like https://name-123.netlify.app.
  3. Claim the site in your account, or it is deleted after a short time. Netlify says files over 10 MB can stall on Drop. If the upload hangs on the OpenCV file, use Option A.

4. Install on the Galaxy Tab A11

  1. Connect the tablet to Wi-Fi. Open Chrome (not Samsung Internet) and type the app address from step 3.
  2. The loading screen appears ("Starting Python … Loading OpenCV …"). The first time it takes 20–60 s.
  3. Chrome asks "Allow … to use your camera?": tap Allow (or "Allow while using the app").
  4. Wait for the message "App saved on this tablet – works offline now".
  5. Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install (or tap the "Install app" banner if it appears).
  6. Close Chrome. Open the app from the new GL121123 icon on the home screen. It opens full screen, without an address bar.
  7. Test offline: switch on flight mode and open the app again. It must start normally.
Recommended tablet settings:
  • Settings → Battery → Background usage limits: do not put Chrome to sleep.
  • Settings → Display → Screen timeout: 10 min. The app also keeps the screen on while it is open.
  • Auto-rotate: either orientation works. Landscape is best (picture left, results right).

5. First start + camera set-up (Setup tab)

  1. Camera: the rear camera starts automatically. In Setup → 1. Camera you can see e.g. "Camera gives 3264×2448 px". If the wrong camera is shown, choose the rear camera in the list. Leave resolution at Highest. If one inspection takes more than about 6 s, choose 2560×1920 (and calibrate again).
  2. Place a part on the pad and wait until the picture is sharp (if it stays blurred, tap Auto focus + exposure or use the focus slider). When the part is sharp, tap Lock focus + exposure so the picture cannot change between parts. If the tablet does not support locking, it says so. That is OK with a backlight. Use the brightness slider, if shown, so the pad is white but not "burnt".
  3. Image check: tap Check image. Aim for a background of 180–245, white-clipped under 20 %, and the text "Part found".
  4. Inspection area: tap Set area (under the picture) and drag a box around the place where parts will lie, about 1 cm bigger than the part on all sides. Tap Done. Anything outside the box is ignored. Zoom shows only the area, bigger.
Want to try the app before the hardware is ready? Use Setup → Demo mode. It simulates a camera and parts. Tap Next part (demo) and the auto-inspection runs. Switch demo mode off again for real use.

6. Calibration (mm per pixel) – required

First enter the master's true values: Training tab → 1. Master true values. Measure the master carefully and type all 7 values. You need them for method A and for training.

See docs/GL121123_measurement_definitions.png for exactly where each length is measured from and to.

Method A – with the master (recommended)

  1. Place the master in the middle of the area and wait until the banner shows "PART READY" or the countdown.
  2. Setup → Measure master → set scale. The app shows the new mm/px. Then continue with training (step 7).

Method B – printed checkerboard

  1. Print docs/calibration_checkerboard_10mm_A4.pdf at 100 % / actual size. Check one square = 10.00 mm with a vernier.
  2. Lay it flat on the pad (switch the pad off if the paper glows through), where the part lies.
  3. Enter the lens height above the table (approximately), then tap Calibrate with checkerboard. The app also tells you if the tablet is tilted.

The Inspect tab shows a yellow warning while the app is not calibrated, or when the camera resolution has changed since calibration.

7. Accuracy training (required, 10 minutes)

A phone or tablet lens sees the part from one point, so a part near the edge of the picture looks slightly different from one in the middle. Training measures the master 10–20 times at different positions. From that, the app learns the exact camera height and centre, corrects every measurement, and tells you how accurate each parameter really is.

  1. Training tab. Check that the true values are filled in.
  2. Switch on Auto-capture every time the master is placed.
  3. Place the master, take your hand away and wait. After 2 s the banner shows "CAPTURED 1". Move the master to a new position and wait again. Repeat 12–20 times. Cover the centre, all 4 corners and the edges, turned ±20°, with the thread pointing left and pointing right. Blue dots show where you already captured.
  4. Switch auto-capture off and tap Analyse accuracy. On the tablet this takes about 0.5–2 minutes.
  5. Read the result table:
    VerdictMeaning
    GOODError (6σ) < 10 % of the tolerance: reliable.
    ACCEPTABLE10–30 %: usable; parts very near a limit can be judged wrong. Recheck those manually.
    NOT CAPABLE> 30 %: do not trust this parameter. Keep the manual gauge for it.
    Red or yellow messages under the table explain problems in plain words, for example "images not stable", "master moved", or "captures only in the centre".
  6. If the result is valid, tap Apply to inspection. Undo last training goes back to the previous calibration.

8. Daily use (operator)

  1. Open the GL121123 app. Wait for the loading screen to finish (5–20 s). Type your name under Operator once.
  2. Check: the banner shows PLACE PART and there is no yellow warning.
  3. Morning check: place the master once. All values must be OK and close to the master's true values. If not, see Troubleshooting.
  4. For each part: place it in the yellow area, then take your hand out. The banner shows HOLD STILL, then a countdown 2.0 → 0.0 s, then INSPECTING, then a big OK or NG.
  5. On NG the failing parameters are written in the red banner and shown red in the table (LOW / HIGH / NOT FOUND), with a beep. A photo of the NG part is saved automatically.
  6. Remove the part. The banner returns to PLACE PART, ready for the next part.
  7. Inspect now starts a measurement immediately, without the 2 s wait.
The bar under each value shows where the value sits within the tolerance: left edge = lower limit, right edge = upper limit. Values close to an edge mean the process is drifting.

9. Changing length / tolerance (Settings tab)

  1. Settings → password (default 1234; change it at the bottom of Settings).
  2. Edit nominal, tol − and tol + for each parameter. Untick On to stop checking a parameter. Values marked * came from "unspecified tolerance ABS00002": Ø14 ±0.2, angle ±0.5°, drop ±0.3. Confirm these with Quality.
  3. Offset is set by training. Change it by hand only if you know why.
  4. Also in Settings: wait time (default 2 s), frames averaged, which photos are saved, and beep.
  5. Tap Save settings.

10. Reports / Excel (History tab)

  • Shows all inspections: today, last 7 days or all. Tap Photo on an NG line to see the saved picture with the values.
  • Export Excel creates an .xlsx with every part and every value. Red cells are out of tolerance. A Summary sheet shows yield, mean, standard deviation and Cpk per parameter. You can Share it (WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive …) or save it to Downloads. Export CSV works the same way.
  • Data stays on the tablet until you tap Delete history (password needed). Export weekly.

11. Backup, updates, second tablet

  • Backup: Settings → Save backup file saves the calibration, training, tolerances and password to Downloads. After a tablet reset, use Restore backup.
  • Update the app: upload the changed files to the same GitHub repository (Add file → Upload files). Then open the app on the tablet once with internet. It shows "App updated – close and reopen". Calibration and history are kept.
  • Second tablet: install from the same address (step 4) and restore the backup. If the stand or tablet is different, do the calibration and training again on that station.
  • Do not use "Clear data" for Chrome or the app in Android settings. That deletes the calibration and history. Make a backup first.

12. Accuracy – what to expect with the tablet camera

The tablet camera has a small lens and a cheaper sensor than the Daheng 20 MP camera. With a good backlight and training, expect about:

ParameterToleranceExpectedUse
Bend angle 41.5°±0.5°±0.1°reliable
Thread length 17.5+0.5/0±0.05–0.1reliable
Ø14±0.2±0.03–0.06mostly reliable
Drop 37.7±0.3±0.08–0.15usable – see training verdict
Length to bend 59+0.3/0±0.08–0.15borderline – see training verdict
Shoulder 49.2+0.2/0±0.08–0.15borderline – see training verdict
Ø12±0.05±0.03–0.05not reliable – keep the snap gauge

The training verdict on your station is the real answer. Use the app as a 100 % screening check for thread, angle, Ø14 and drop. For any parameter the training marks NOT CAPABLE, keep the manual gauge.

13. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Loading screen stays / "Engine could not start"Close the app completely (recent apps → swipe away) and open it again. If it repeats, open once with internet. The tablet needs about 500 MB of free memory: close other apps.
"Camera blocked"Android Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Camera → Allow. In the app, tap the lock icon / site settings → Camera → Allow.
Front camera shownSetup → camera list → choose the rear ("back" / "environment") camera.
Banner always "REMOVE HAND"Something dark touches the yellow area border: a hand, cable, marks on the paper, or the pad edge. Make the area smaller, or clean the surface.
Banner stays "HOLD STILL"The tablet or stand vibrates, or the light flickers. Fix the stand. Settings → stillness limit can be raised from 1.5 to 3.
Never starts – "PLACE PART" with the part thereToo little contrast. Use the backlight, or Settings → Threshold = Fixed with a value of about 100.
"Part end not found" / NOT FOUND valuesThe part is partly outside the area, there is dirt on the pad, the part is shiny or grey (too much light from above), or the picture is blurred. Run Check image.
Yellow toast "Ø14 reads x % off"The tablet moved or zoom changed. Calibrate and train again.
"RECALIBRATE – resolution changed"Select the same resolution as during calibration, or calibrate again.
Same part gives different valuesLock focus and exposure; use the backlight; make the stand rigid; raise "frames averaged" to 5.
Inspection takes more than 6 sSetup → resolution 2560×1920 (then calibrate and train again). Close other apps.
Excel does not open on the tabletInstall Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets from the Play Store, or share the file to the PC.
